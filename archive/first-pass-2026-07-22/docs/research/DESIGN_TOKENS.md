# Design tokens

## Palette

- ROOSA pink: `#ef83b6` (derived from current-site `rgb(241,135,182)`, tuned for the new system).
- Deep pink: `#c92f78`.
- Soft pink: `#ffd8e9`.
- Paper: `#fffaf4`.
- White: `#ffffff`.
- Graphite: `#241f22`.
- Muted: `#756b70`.
- Border: `rgba(36,31,34,.16)`.
- Impact green: `#204f45`.

## Typography

- Display: Geist, with tight tracking and high contrast through weight, not decorative display fonts.
- Body: Geist.
- Display range: `clamp(3.7rem, 10vw, 9rem)`.
- Section heading range: `clamp(2.4rem, 6vw, 5.5rem)`.

## Shape

- Buttons use pill radii.
- Product media uses 4:5 paper panels with one cut corner, not generic rounded cards.
- Dividers use repeating perforation patterns.

## Motion

- Fast: 160ms.
- Medium: 320ms.
- Slow: 700ms.
- Standard easing: cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1).
- Narrative easing: cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1).
