Coca-Cola’s Classic Moments

1886

The Birth of Coca-Cola

Coca-Cola was invented by Dr. John S. Pemberton in Atlanta, Georgia. What started as a soda fountain drink at Jacob’s Pharmacy has since become a global icon of refreshment.

1915

The Iconic Bottle Design

The contour bottle design was introduced to distinguish Coca-Cola from imitators. Its unique shape became an instant classic, symbolizing the brand’s distinct identity and is still recognized today.

1931

Coca-Cola and Santa Claus

Coca-Cola’s holiday advertisements featuring a jolly Santa Claus solidified the modern image of Santa. This campaign connected Coca-Cola with joy, celebration, and the holiday spirit.

1971

I’d Like to Buy the World a Coke

One of the most famous ads in history, this campaign captured the spirit of harmony and unity, showcasing people from around the world singing together on a hilltop in Italy, all sharing a Coke.
