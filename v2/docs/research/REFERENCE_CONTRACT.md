# ROOSA v2 reference contract

This file fixes the implementation hierarchy. A lower item may supply only the
responsibility named here; it may not redesign a higher item.

1. **GiveWell** — exact homepage skeleton, section order, desktop dimensions,
   editorial rhythm, typography scale, tabs, pinned vision sequence, statistics
   grid, and footer composition.
2. **Soma / CartGenie** — product-card geometry, product gallery/detail layout,
   whitespace, product hover image, store page, and editorial-commerce grouping.
3. **Bovist** — quantity field, add-to-cart and buy-now controls, cart state,
   stock/disabled states, related products, and Shopify-facing data boundaries.
4. **Coca-Cola 3D concept** — the single hero product image and its scroll-linked
   rotation/translation. It does not determine the rest of the page.
5. **Sitasys Interactions** — clipped text/image reveals and restrained layered
   movement. It does not determine page structure or brand styling.

ROOSA content replaces source content. Verified business values remain explicit
bracketed placeholders. Source-specific charity, cosmetics, furniture, and soft
drink styling/content must not leak into the finished ROOSA copy.
