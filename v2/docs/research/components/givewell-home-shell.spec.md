- Text and media substitutions must operate on the copied source DOM.
- Add the Soma product-options module between the GiveWell team and vision zones.

- Empower tabs auto-cycle every 4000ms and restart after click.
- Vision marquee, pinned expanding central image, lateral side-image movement.
- Stats scramble once over 2s; do not animate fabricated numeric values.

## Responsive behavior

  when reduced motion or narrow viewport makes it unsuitable.
